## Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-12_

**Vibe** is a local-first transcription app. It does not collect telemetry or analytics, and it does not transmit your data on its own.

______________________________________________________________________

### 1. No Telemetry, No Analytics

**Vibe** has no telemetry, no analytics service, no account, and no tracking identifier. Usage events and error reports are not sent anywhere.

______________________________________________________________________

### 2. Data Stays on Your Device

Audio, video, transcripts, settings, log files, and diagnostics reports stay on your device. They leave only when you choose to share them, for example by copying a file or attaching it to a report.

Logs and diagnostics are local artefacts. **Vibe** never uploads them automatically.

______________________________________________________________________

### 3. Network Access

**Vibe** contacts the network only when a feature needs it:

- downloading models
- checking for updates
- opening a page or link you chose
- calling an AI provider you configured for summaries or other optional features

Each request goes only to the service that feature needs. **Vibe** adds no tracking of its own.

______________________________________________________________________

### 4. External Links

**Vibe** and its website link to external sites such as [GitHub](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/vibe). Those sites apply their own privacy policies.

______________________________________________________________________

### 5. Open Source

**Vibe** is open source. The code is available at [github.com/thewh1teagle/vibe](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/vibe).

______________________________________________________________________

### 6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Updates are published on the website and in the repository.
