# Transcribe from your phone

Vibe can record on a phone and transcribe on this computer. The phone opens a small web app after scanning a code; nothing is installed from an app store. Keep Vibe open while recording.

## Enable the handoff

1. Open **Settings → Phone**.
1. Turn on **Allow phone recordings**.
1. Choose **Pair a phone**, then **Show code**.

## Pair the phone

1. Point the phone camera at the QR code.
1. Open the page it offers and keep it open.
1. The device appears under **Paired devices** once pairing completes.

Pairing survives restarts of the app and the phone browser. Each browser profile is a separate device.

## Record and transcribe

1. Record on the phone page.
1. The audio travels through the relay end-to-end encrypted; this computer receives it, transcribes it, and sends the transcript back.
1. The transcript also lands in Recents on the computer.

## Manage devices

- **Revoke** blocks that device and interrupts an active handoff.
- **Create new code** replaces the invitation. Already paired phones keep access.
- **Revoke all** makes every phone scan a new code.
